Rope Skipping Count

A black-and-white counting story to read with children. Follow the arrows from box to box. In each number box, tap Count with me to hear the swish-thwack of the rope and count the skips!

Practice Skipping Pad

The playground

We are in the playground: two friends turning the long rope, and one friend right in the middle, ready to jump! The rope goes swish, swish through the air.

Ready, jump!

Look at the rope curve right under the sneakers! Bend your knees, listen for the rhythm, and spring up off your toes!

One

Hop! We jumped once over the rope. Clean jump!Count: 1. One skip!

Two

Hop, hop! We skipped 2 times. The rope sings: swish-thwack, swish-thwack!Count: 1, 2. Two skips!

Three

Hop, hop, hop! Three springy skips! Up in the air, over the rope, and land light as a feather.Count: 1, 2, 3. Three skips!

Four

Hop, hop, hop, hop! Four skips in a row without catching our feet. Keep the steady beat!Count: 1, 2, 3, 4. Four skips!

Five

Hop, hop, hop, hop, hop! Five whole skips! The rope spins round and round like a bicycle wheel!Count: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. Five skips!

After five

After 5, we keep on jumping! Six, seven, eight, nine, ten... faster and faster! How many skips can we do before we tumble down laughing? We don't know yet!Count: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 … and keep on skipping!
